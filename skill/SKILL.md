# Investment-master research

Use source-grounded methods, not impersonated investor opinions. This bundle is a reviewable research specification. Read `reviews/source-acceptance-ledger.json` for current bounded source receipts; bundle/implementation/historical transport acceptance remain separate; no model run, empirical validation, installation, publishing or trading is implied.

## Select the evidence mode

- Current research: use the current method registry and only the relevant current dossier/evidence files. Separate latest statements, historical practice and your application.
- Historical replay: obtain original investor-method facts only from the isolated `historical-methods/` package; add the independently frozen company packet, reviewed selected procedural cards and fixed single-view schema through the explicit transport allowlist. Never give a decision worker this entrypoint, current dossiers, catalogue, cases, review scores, current holdings, outcome paths or result files. Read `references/historical-isolation.md` outside the decision worker. A pending historical allowlist is a blocker.
- A request for an actual named investor's view requires a matching original statement; otherwise describe a hypothetical application of the documented method.

## Research sequence

1. Verify issuer, listing, class, currency, share unit and dated conversions. Record the question, horizon, cutoff, lawful sources and gaps. Do not substitute ADRs, ordinary/preferred shares or another listing silently.
2. Inventory original evidence and admissibility before interpretation. Keep provenance, timing, claim kind, source/version, period and unit separate. Null is missing. Estimates and assumptions remain labelled.
3. Freeze observable instrument/business traits and data availability before recommendations. Use `references/routing.md` and `registry/current-method-registry.json`. Preserve a requested master, including a bounded abstention. A source-only named-master request triggers a bounded original-evidence audit under the source/holdings assignment; never silently simulate a catalogue persona. Three slots require distinct master identities and a genuinely different challenge; do not fill missing eligibility with celebrity names or technology enthusiasm. Do not select any security route until its company facts are frozen.
4. Use the complete preserved finterm backbone in `vendor/finterm/`: choose financial-analysis, memo-review or thesis-evaluation; route an ordinary 5–12 cards; load only relevant modules and retain supported/challenged/unresolved outputs. Do not run its bundled program without separate execution review. The backbone is analytical and does not issue trade recommendations; decision scenario extensions live outside it.
5. Read only each selected modular method file under `methods/` and the exact original evidence needed to verify material claims. Require method scope, necessary inputs, falsifiers and attribution limits. Current-source rules cannot backfill historical eligibility.
6. Complete the source/holdings assignment in `references/source-holdings-audit.md` when manager practice is relevant. A filing proves disclosed facts, not private intent, full wealth, leverage, exact trading cost or realized profit. Preserve valid tensions and not-comparable pairs.
7. Produce quantitative analysis through the unchanged accepted R2 contract when applicable. Read `references/quantitative-output.md` and `compatibility/r2/`. New formulas are specifications pending separate unit/domain/security review; do not execute upstream formulas. Missing adapter results remain missing.
8. Emit the separate typed research wrapper in `schemas/research-result.v0.1.schema.json`, linked by artifact hash to the unchanged adapter result. Preserve four panels: quality, valuation, evidence coverage and uncertainty. Keep per-master mechanisms, horizons, abstentions and disagreements visible. Require the fail-closed semantic preflight in `schemas/semantic-preflight-contract.v0.1.json`, including exact route/view IDs, artifact hashes, actual model identity and separate ticker-level original-view/holding status. No universal score, vote, averaged incompatible targets or calibrated-probability claim.

## Hard stops

Abstain on unresolved instrument identity, unsupported method authority, missing material inputs, post-cutoff/unknown-date facts in a replay, unknown actual model identity, or unreviewed calculation semantics. Keep the unaffected research branches useful. Stop a stable task after at most three corrective rework rounds; return remaining defects and exact blockers without claiming completion. No external sends, credential handling, restricted-source redistribution, live integration or account actions are authorized by this Skill.

For a configured three-view research run, use three independent fresh model contexts, each with one assigned lens, frozen company packet and selected backbone cards. Each returns one view; assemble the three-slot wrapper only afterward. A bounded on-demand original-evidence audit can return its audit artifact without forcing three generated opinions.
