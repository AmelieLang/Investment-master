## Operational fit and pricing boundary

ARK is a candidate mechanism lens when a business actually depends on technology cost/capability shifts, adoption or platform value capture and the necessary inputs exist. A sector label alone is insufficient. Baron is a candidate growth-business lens when differentiation, management execution and productive reinvestment can be investigated. The data/workflow case can only supply a separately labelled hypothetical mechanism question.

Both require company-level funding, dilution, execution and entry-value discipline. This extension does not recover a native discount rate, universal growth threshold, target horizon, position-sizing algorithm or buy/sell score. Missing valuation inputs must yield no numerical value. Industry opportunity, customer surplus, vendor revenue and owner cash flow remain different quantities.

No personal Laffont methodology is added. The existing bounded Gerstner rules are unchanged. No new investor names or holdings-based credibility substitutes are introduced.
