# Registry and heterogeneous dossier normalization

`registry/current-method-registry.json` links the canonical source catalogue to eleven requested curated method modules. Canonical catalogue coverage is 121 entities, not 121 authenticated experts. Catalogue entries enable zero automatic expert routes. The ten-core roster and Leopold extension remain distinct; source identity and method authenticity are different gates.

Heterogeneous formats are retained in `current-library/` as evidence snapshots after stream-final notification. Normalized `methods/` documents do not replace or discard raw evidence. The normalization receipt maps source IDs, claim IDs and rule IDs to original relative file/JSON-pointer locators, input file hashes, transformed publication/authority classifications and every excluded historical rule. Each substantive rule retains its original record and the current source-register evidence. A normalized field is a translation of schema, not a new factual claim.

Fundamental claims use A/B/C access labels, explicit claims and operationalized rules. A direct-original A item maps to P1 for what it actually establishes; original-publisher transcript B may map P2, while metadata-only/inaccessible B remains limited and secondary compilation C never becomes primary proof. Tech sources are keyed objects; source type, named authors, process scope and pre-cutoff flags stay separate. Macro uses source visibility bounds and native-principle/action pairs: retain caveats around republished sources and do not relabel analyst operationalization native. Leopold uses P/T labels directly and stays emerging/thematic with no security-specific pre-trade valuation claim.

Current sources, holding snapshots, selected cases, contradiction pairs, uncertainty, search logs and denominators remain in the current evidence library. No files from that library enter the historical worker export. Internal filesystem cache locators are omitted from portable catalogue copies; public repo/commit/path/source hashes and independent original evidence IDs remain. No raw cache, full book, restricted terminal report, private URL, credential or private user message is packaged.

New methods extend this registry through a separately bounded evidence assignment and review receipt. Alias resolution must not silently merge Paul/Benjamin Graham, a pseudonym and a named person, a firm and its founder, or team research and individual authorship. Preserve catalogue defects and pending corrections visibly rather than manufacturing a complete reconciliation.

## Applicability polarity

For every fundamental rule, preserve positive fit and excluded fit in separate `application_scope.applies_to` and `application_scope.does_not_apply_to` fields. These scope descriptions come from the source auditor’s adaptation and are explicitly labelled, separately from the investor’s documented method fact. Historical prose renders both labels. A mandatory exact comparison against the accepted method-packet rule ID/hash rejects reversed, unlabeled, missing or duplicated polarity; a shape schema alone cannot prove that direction is faithful. `reviews/scope-polarity-QA.round2.json` records thirteen positive and sixty-five actually rejected static mutations.
