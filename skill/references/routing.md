# Evidence-aware fit router

This is our design. It is not any investor's native classifier, point system or proprietary algorithm. Catalogue inclusion, repository popularity, a past holding and a technology label confer no eligibility.

## Freeze inputs before opinions

The route receipt contains exact security identity, packet hash, cutoff and horizon; observable asset/business traits with supporting fact IDs; necessary-input coverage; eligible candidate rule IDs and method hashes; excluded candidates and reasons; requested master IDs; exactly three distinct view slots when the task requires three; and a mechanism-diversity explanation. Generate the receipt without access to any opinion, return path, hindsight narrative or prior reviewer score. Hash/freeze it before asking the model for views. Never reroute to obtain a desired conclusion.

Gate in this order:
1. Asset/instrument: corporate equity, bank/insurer, credit/distressed claim, rates/FX, commodity/future, index/portfolio, token or private venture. No equity DCF transplanted into a bond or token.
2. Observable economics/data: recurring cash, reinvestment/unit economics, pre-profit adoption, cyclicality, financing stress, legal catalyst, macro transmission, or technical/systematic data. A sector label is only a descriptor.
3. Source-backed method: correct speaker/team/mandate, accepted evidence and necessary inputs. Pending review means a candidate, not an executable expert route.
4. Main mechanism: normally one or two strongest fits, judged ordinally with explicit reasons. Avoid invented precision or weighting. Keep quality and price questions separate.
5. Meaningfully different challenge: test value/price, financing survival, macro/cycle, accounting, implementation or falsification through a different causal lens. Similar value checklists are not three independent votes.
6. Requested master: always preserve its requested slot or explicit requested-method abstention; supplement suitable lenses without erasing the request. Three-slot completion may contain abstentions. Do not manufacture a method because the user requested a name.

Typical mechanisms, conditional on accepted sources:
- Durable cash equity: owner cash/competence/capital allocation, challenged by price and reinvestment limits.
- Profitable technology: concrete retention, distribution, capital efficiency and customer value, challenged by price-implied duration and financing. Both value and growth lenses can reject or accept it.
- Pre-profit innovation: adoption, costs, customer ROI, cash needs and dilution; a historical fund-specific scenario does not become a universal investor model.
- Cyclical equity: normalized demand/cash and funded survival plus supply/cycle constraints.
- AI-supply exposure: verify each customer/supplier edge, deliverable capacity and economic rent; technical capability alone does not establish shareholder value.
- Macro-sensitive corporate equity: use regime/financing questions as context, not a substitute for security-level valuation.
- Banks/insurers, credit and special instruments: sector/claim-specific accounting, legal priority and funding first.

## Historical limits

Historical eligibility is rule-level. Rich current methods are not automatically available before the cutoff. A one-rule recovered generic fund method is question-only until an accepted source set supports the material mechanism being asked. Team-authored Coatue research must be labelled Coatue team; it is not a personal Laffont algorithm. A requested Laffont historical personal-method slot may abstain. Retain Leopold as an emerging thematic/financing-risk extension, never elevate it to an established master or infer an unverified proprietary return record.

A historical experiment’s requested security universe is an acquisition target list, not a route table. Wait for frozen company facts. If fewer than three distinct eligible methods can address material questions, return explicit incomplete/abstaining slots and reasons. Never fill the gap from source-only catalogue names.
