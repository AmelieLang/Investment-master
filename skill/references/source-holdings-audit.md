# Source and holdings research assignment contract

Machine-readable assignment: `../schemas/source-holdings-audit-assignment.v0.1.json`. The search is bounded and its denominator is reported. Do not promise all lifetime writings, private positions, full books, complete social feeds or proprietary models.

## Required source research

For each of the ten core dossiers, record identity/mandate periods and aliases; find/read at least three substantive items from at least two source families, including direct-method primary/authorized evidence and practice evidence. Record found/read/unavailable/excluded counts and exact missing gates. Leopold is a separate user-confirmed emerging extension, not an amendment of the frozen ten. Other catalogue perspectives stay source-only until independently audited.

Each substantive claim needs a stable ID, direct statement/regulatory holding/reported transaction/inferred method/reconstruction/application/assumption/outcome kind, P1/P2/S1/S2/U provenance, T0/T1/T2/TU timing, separate event/publication/archive/retrieval dates, source/version, exact locator, short paraphrase or lawful excerpt, preserved qualifications and what it cannot establish. Original-language wording remains alongside translation where investment meaning depends on it. GitHub persona text is implementation lineage, not original-investor proof.

For living practicing managers with lawful public disclosures, seek at least two compatible dated snapshots and two tested statement/practice pairs, ideally apparent fit and apparent mismatch. If no legal public holdings exist, document the gap and substitute verified practice without calling it a full portfolio. Separate personal investment, reporting manager, fund, share class and joint/team discretion.

## Comparison procedure

Before scoring any tension, verify same decision-maker, security/class/vehicle, position side, currency, report/event period, decision/horizon and stated qualifiers. Keep actual public filing time separate from quarter-end. Examine amendments, instrument conversions, options, private assets, partial reporting and changes in mandate. Potential explanations (hedges, redemptions, taxes, changed facts, employee discretion) are hypotheses unless sourced.

Outcomes are consistent, apparent_tension, resolved_change, attribution_uncertain, not_comparable or unresolved. A missing long holding proves neither a short nor dislike. A present holding proves neither unlimited conviction nor profit. Never compute realized profit from snapshot market-value differences. Distinguish security total return, fund return, trade P&L, manager self-report and interval proxy. A proxy requires bounds, costs/corporate actions/currency/fees/leverage/benchmark assumptions or remains unknown.

## Review and correction

Return full evidence artifacts plus a concise coverage ledger and contradictions table. Source minimums may be reported met by the worker; independent research-ready acceptance stays pending until the designated reviewer/root records an exact receipt. Max three corrective rounds for this stable assignment; after that return unresolved defects. No permissions to scrape private material, upload full copyrighted sources, expand credentials or send third-party messages are granted.
