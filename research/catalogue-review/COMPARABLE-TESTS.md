# Comparable replay design and evidence limits

This is a source and design recap of the accepted V1 experiment, not a new experiment. Numerical model outputs, historical returns and actual V2 opinions are outside this candidate.

## Common design

Decision information cutoff: 2015-12-31 16:00 America/New_York, after the close. The three cases were NVDA, TSLA and VIPS. Every method and control received the same frozen company packet and query for a case. Filings were admitted by publication date rather than fiscal-period label. Later stock outcomes and the authentic-comparator evidence were withheld during initial analysis.

Three method conditions plus the no-skill control produced 12 first-pass cells. The accepted review confirms 12 valid first passes, no hard-invalid cells and no replacement reruns. These are acceptance/coverage counts, not return or accuracy scores. Repository software was not installed or run end-to-end: the test wrapped or composed pinned method text in fresh contexts.

The explicitly selected backend was gpt-6.1-sol with xhigh reasoning. The exact backend snapshot, temperature and seed were not exposed. There was one first pass per cell; no repeated-run stability trial or anonymized control was executed.

## Frozen conditions

| Condition | Composition | Native output boundary |
|---|---|---|
| finterm | Buffett financial-analysis entrypoint with exactly 12 complete router-selected cards per company | Research checks; Munger is corroborating influence, not an independent scored lens. Native scope can abstain from aggregate investment action and numerical intrinsic value. |

| monarchjuno | Fixed Buffett and Munger lenses plus generic company-analysis workflow | A composed research method, not one native investor valuation engine. |
| No-skill control | Same query and company packet with no investor skill | Diagnostic baseline; not a project-quality score. |

## C5 method fidelity

Five fixed dimensions score method application (30), arithmetic/security/currency/period consistency (25), thesis/countercase/accounting evidence (20), reproducible assumptions/sensitivity or justified abstention (15), and calibration/falsifiability/native-scope compliance (10). B is the mean of a finalist's three required valid company scores; C5 = B/20. A combined score is defined only after all required cells are valid, using 85% unrounded static source base plus 15% B. This recap does not republish B, C5 or combined empirical scores.

Decision-use of post-cutoff facts, fabricated quotes/sources, and material currency/share/ADS/split mismatches invalidate a cell. INVALID is not zero, cannot be offset by other dimensions, and cannot be silently removed from the mean. Correct missing-data or native-scope abstention earns method credit without inventing a stock score or recommendation.

C5 measures adherence to this evidence/method rubric. It does not measure agreement with a real investor, correct stock decisions, returns, alpha or general model uplift. A strong no-skill control, unequal native output scope, subjective grading and rubric ceilings limit comparative inference.

## Separate original investor view cross check

A matched comparator requires the company, public date, decision type and horizon to match. A primary dated investor statement is stronger than second-hand attendee notes. A quality comment does not establish a valuation or trade decision. A 13F establishes disclosed exposure at the report date, not intent, purchase price, personal authorship or a universal investment rule.

The accepted bounded search found no strict pre-cutoff primary stock-decision comparator across 12 actual investor–company cells. Evidence coverage is 0/12, while the equity-alignment percentage is undefined. That is a search-coverage result, not 0% agreement, 0% accuracy or proof that those investors never commented.

One shared grade-C source concerns Munger's Tesla business-risk discussion. It is attendee reporting rather than a verified verbatim transcript. Narrow risk components in two method outputs were partially compatible with it; this is not two independent confirmations. It establishes no stock price, action, horizon, disclosed holding or December 31 opinion.

## Further comparable tests remain proposals

The following are reproducible test requirements, not claims of executed experiments: rerun fixed inputs in fresh contexts to measure variance; use anonymized and no-skill controls; test missing fields and conflicting evidence; perturb units, currencies and publication dates; hold out companies; verify source-induced mechanisms against primary dated material; and evaluate post-decision outcomes only in a separate ex-post ledger after any decision and execution mapping are frozen.

Current models and 2026 method packages can encode later knowledge even with a frozen 2015 packet. The three selected stocks have hindsight and survivorship bias. V2's historical transport, numerical runtime, security-level comparisons and model opinions require their own acceptance. These are unfinished gates. No V2 investment opinion, native-program execution, realized trade or successful returns backtest is claimed here.
