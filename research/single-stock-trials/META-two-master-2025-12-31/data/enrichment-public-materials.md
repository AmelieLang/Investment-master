## E1. Original earnings-call product detail and capital-allocation Q&A

Source: [Meta Q3 2025 earnings call transcript](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/META-Q3-2025-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf). Event date 2025-10-29, shown in transcript; exact later PDF-upload time not independently established. Primary management testimony. Sections: printed pages 2 and 6-7, and Doug Anmuth Q&A on printed pages 10-11. Not included in the base.

- E1a, management-reported operating observations: AI recommendation changes increased Facebook time spent by 5% and Threads by 10%. Lattice's app-ad deployment produced nearly 3% conversion gain for that objective. These are scoped management measurements, not independently audited universal conversion improvements.
- E1b, monetization scope: end-to-end automated ad tools handled a $60b annualized revenue run rate. This is ad revenue running through tools, not $60b of incremental standalone AI revenue; it can overlap existing revenue.
- E1c, forward-looking judgment: Susan Li argued additional compute could be used profitably in the core business. Mark Zuckerberg saw surplus capacity as usable for ads/recommendations, potentially external services, or future demand. Overshoot was acknowledged. No promised ROI, margin, date of payback or completed external-compute business was established.

Limitation: Management's incentives and experimental definitions matter; core-ad efficiency gains do not establish the return on all frontier-model investment.

- E2c, external valuation claim: the article attributed a comparison around 21x 2027 estimated earnings for Meta and Alphabet. This is the analyst's dated estimate-based comparison, not a verified current multiple, the supplied cutoff-price multiple, or an independent valuation conclusion.

Limitation: Full model, assumptions and forecast series are unavailable. A historical analogy and brokerage rating do not quantify loss probabilities or justify importing a multiple into either method.

## Conflicting hypotheses to test independently

- Core ad economics provide profitable uses for additional compute, and temporary investment pressure may accompany a longer growth runway (E1 and E3).
- Frontier-model spending may outrun monetizable returns and resemble prior expensive projects despite healthy core advertising (E2).

Neither hypothesis is resolved by these materials alone. Identify which observations bear on each, what is inference, what is still unmeasured, and whether each investor method's own conclusion changes. Preserve disagreement between methods.
